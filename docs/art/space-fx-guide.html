Status badges

The twelve ability and action glyphs, redrawn as filled chunky art for SR_SPACE_FX.badge. The pop runs ease.outBack; under reduced motion it lands instantly.

Lock-on marks

The binary read for a busy sky: rotating thin arcs = acquiring; the instant a lock completes they snap to four still corner ticks and a solid diamond, with one pop ring at the flip. Gold is the player's lock; hazard orange is a lock on the player. No glow, no fill — it sits over the fight without covering it.

Backdrop and rocks

The quiet world: void, a whisper of nebula and a far star layer on parallax (the camera pans to show the depth), cel asteroids baked once each. Low contrast on purpose — the fight carries the colour.

The stress scene: the hc3 brawl

16 ships (two marine Grafs, fighters, Commandant Vex, motes and drones, the reactor), ~150 live bolts, 6 missiles with tracking smoke, chaff, a kill every few seconds, boarding running — the §8 budget case. The counter is the whole FX draw per frame (update + every layer and body), p50 / p95 over the last 240 frames. Budget: ≤ 8 ms at p95, DPR 2.
