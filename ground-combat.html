Steal the Cross

Dustfall keeps one FT-4 Cross on the pad behind the sheriff’s HQ. The FT-4 is built for frontier sheriffs — cheap, reliable, made to scare off smugglers. Strap more guns and missiles to it and it becomes rather useful in a pinch. Sheriff Reeve enforces Hegemony law here. We enforce ours.

Get Sera Kest to the Cross. She carries a sidearm and not much else — keep bodies between her and the shooting. Once she reaches the pad she needs two full rounds to hotwire it out from under them. Then walk the squad back to the Graf and lift — as long as no lawman is standing on the ramp.

Plan every rebel’s order, then execute — everyone moves at once. Move keeps your gun up · Sprint goes twice as far but you can’t shoot and you’re harder to hit · Hold braces your aim and watches a lane — anyone crossing it eats a snap shot. Keep to barrels and wagons: cover turns hits into misses. The town hasn’t noticed you yet. Anything worth taking — tills, strongboxes, lockers — has to be looted on the ground, by hand. Akli rifles run dirty: a roll of 1 jams the action.
