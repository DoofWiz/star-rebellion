Take Out Instructor

Commandant Dral Vex trains every Hegemony fighter pilot in this sector, and his cadets leave the academy shooting straight. Right now he's running a live exercise in the Veray Drift with four green cadets and no escort. Kill the instructor and the Hegemony's next class of pilots dies with him.

OBJECTIVE — Eliminate Commandant Vex. His cadets are green; expect them to break when he falls.
Battlespace: drag / WASD to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, minimap to jump. Double-click any fighter (on the field or the roster) for its pilot dossier and ship readout. Planning: pick a ship — its dial opens beside it. Execution: ships move by initiative; yours get an action ring. Locks are acquired on targets inside your firing cone at lock range, stack +2 to-hit per level (missiles also gain guidance), and are lost only when the target ends up behind you. Evade and Repair open deeper options — angle shield segments, patch hull, clear criticals. Attack: the VS panel builds both sides into a live hit chance, then a d20 decides it. Shields cover fore and aft zones; ballistic ignores them. Hull damage inflicts one named critical per attack until someone field-repairs.

Steal the Cross

Dustfall keeps one FT-4 Cross on the pad behind the sheriff’s HQ. The FT-4 is built for frontier sheriffs — cheap, reliable, made to scare off smugglers. Strap more guns and missiles to it and it becomes rather useful in a pinch. Sheriff Reeve enforces Hegemony law here. We enforce ours.

Get Sera Kest to the Cross on the pad at the far north-east of town. She carries a sidearm and not much else — keep bodies between her and the shooting. She needs two rounds at the panel to hotwire it, and while she works, someone else has to release the docking clamps and pull the fuel line before the ship can fly. Then walk the squad back to the Graf and lift.

While the town is quiet you move in real time — right-click and the squad follows, scooping up loot they pass. The law’s sight cones are drawn on the ground; linger in one and the eye above your rebel fills toward a full alarm. Sneak (C) halves your pace and your profile. When the law wakes, time drops into rounds: Move keeps your gun up · Sprint goes far but can’t shoot · Hold braces and overwatches. Cover turns hits into misses — and shreds under fire, so don’t trust a crate forever. Mind the laser turret by the pad: shielded from the front, soft from the flanks, and either side can man it. The red fuel canisters go up if anyone shoots them. Akli rifles jam on a roll of 1. Clear the street and time runs free again — then hit Extract.
