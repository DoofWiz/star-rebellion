Take Out Instructor

Commandant Dral Vex trains every Hegemony fighter pilot in this sector, and his cadets leave the academy shooting straight. Right now he's running a live exercise in the Veray Drift with four green cadets and no escort. Kill the instructor and the Hegemony's next class of pilots dies with him.

OBJECTIVE — Eliminate Commandant Vex. His cadets are green; expect them to break when he falls.

Battlespace: drag / WASD to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, minimap to jump. Each round: plot every ship's maneuver, then execute — ships move one at a time in ascending initiative, and each of your pilots chooses an action at their activation (Lock stacks +8 to-hit per level and arms missiles, Evade, Steady recovers nerve). Guns then fire in descending initiative on a d100 against a target number built from angle, range, cover and pilot nerve. Plasma is soaked by shields; ballistic bypasses shields and can score criticals; missiles need a lock. Rocks block sensor locks and foul firing lines. Watch the cool meter — panicking pilots fly and shoot badly.
